Drift loop

a 128×128 autoregressive loop at 22Hz over a stand-in creature. the clean render (top right) is the anchor; everything else is the loop failing to remember it.


  
Top right: clean render above, drifted output below. Look for the silhouette lagging the
  body, chroma splitting on edges, features snapping to the latent grid. F re-converges it onto a creature that is
  not the one you were watching — in game that fires when you lose line of sight, not on a keypress.
  [ and ] change distance; judge it at 6 blocks, which is where you fight.
